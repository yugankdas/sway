How it works

Two devices. The laptop is the booth. The phone is the instrument.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Open the booth on your laptop and scan its QR code with your phone. Same Wi-Fi, no app to install.

  2. 02

    Move

    Tilt left or right to slide between Deck A and Deck B. Tip it forward to lift the energy, or back to muffle it.

  3. 03

    Mix

    In Auto DJ, Sway waits for the next phrase or downbeat and cuts the outgoing bass so the blend lands cleanly.

Meet the booth

Everything you need to read the mix from across the room. Nothing you don’t.

  • Two decks. Blue for A, yellow for B, each with its track name, time, BPM and waveform.
  • Your own files. Load any MP3 from your laptop. Nothing gets uploaded to a cloud.
  • Hands-on when you want it. Drag the crossfader, or use the arrow keys.
  • Auto DJ. Call the transition with a tilt. Sway picks the moment.
  • Music-aware. Each track is analyzed for downbeats and sections. If analysis isn’t available, Sway falls back to a clean equal-power crossfade.
Open the booth

Two ways to play

Manual

You control the mix directly.

Aphone = faderB
  • The fader goes exactly where your phone points.
  • Tilt halfway for a blend, all the way for a full swap.
  • Energy nudge works in both modes.
VS

Auto DJ

You ask for the transition. Sway picks the musical moment.

  1. Tilt hard to B
  2. Wait for phrase or downbeat
  3. Match BPM, cut bass
  4. Glide to B
  • Waits for a phrase boundary if one is less than 5 seconds away. Otherwise it goes on the next downbeat.
  • Temporarily matches the incoming track when the two tracks are within range.
  • Without analysis, it does a plain equal-power crossfade.
